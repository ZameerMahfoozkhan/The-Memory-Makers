Our Story

Meet the passionate artists behind every frame — a team driven by love, light, and the art of storytelling.

Est. 2013 — Lucknow, India

Crafting Memories, One Frame at a Time

The Memory Makers Photography was born from a simple belief — that every love story deserves to be told beautifully. Founded by Ankit Maurya, our studio has grown from a solo passion project into a full-service photography and filmmaking team trusted by over 1,200 families.

With more than 12 years of experience, we specialize in capturing the authentic emotions that make your celebrations unique. From the nervous excitement before the vows to the joyful tears on the dance floor, we're there to preserve every precious moment.

Our Mission: To create timeless visual narratives that you'll cherish for generations — photographs and films that make you feel, remember, and relive.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your session with us.

What areas do you cover for weddings?

+

We are based in Lucknow but cover weddings across India and internationally. We've shot in over 25 cities including Jaipur, Goa, Delhi, Udaipur, and more. Destination weddings are our specialty!

How far in advance should we book?

+

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance, especially during the wedding season (November–February). Popular dates fill up quickly, so early booking ensures availability.

What is included in a wedding photography package?

+

Our packages typically include full-day coverage, a team of photographers and videographers, professional editing, a cinematic highlight reel, and high-resolution images delivered via an online gallery. Custom packages are available upon request.

How long does it take to receive the final photos and videos?

+

Edited photos are typically delivered within 3-4 weeks. Cinematic wedding films may take 6-8 weeks due to the extensive editing process. We also provide sneak peeks within 48 hours of the event.

Do you offer pre-wedding shoots?

+

Absolutely! Pre-wedding shoots are one of our most popular services. We offer both local and destination pre-wedding shoots at stunning locations of your choice.

Can we customize our package?

+

Yes! Every couple is unique, and so are our packages. We'll work with you to create a custom package that fits your needs, preferences, and budget perfectly.
